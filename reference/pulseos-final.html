Questions, answered.

Is PulseOS a replacement for my doctor?

No. It helps you understand your reports and prepare better questions. Your doctor makes the decisions.

What files can I upload?

PDFs and photos of lab reports. Text is extracted, then explained value by value.

Is the data on this page real?

No. This is a design demo, and every value, doctor, and reminder is sample data.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can export or remove your reports and vitals at any time.
